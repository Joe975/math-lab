(verified exact-equal under order shuffles, 203 instances), and
`seq_can` ≡ `canon_completion(∅)` plus the completion recursion verified
on the committed functions directly; (ii) V_{k+1} ≥ V_k is sound: the
V_k-realizing sequence continues P_{k+1} because rollout's step-k pick
is the argmax, and its tail reappears verbatim as the candidate
f(P_{k+1}); with V_0 ≥ CR(canon) via the candidate f(∅) and V_{n−1} =

(iii) the float caveat is real but bounded: the implemented rules break

stress test over 150 perturbed-exchangeable measures found a genuine
inversion of CR_roll − CR_canon = **−4.4e-15** (roll picked (0,1,2,3),
canon (1,0,3,2) on a measure exchangeable up to ~2e-11), which is noise
9 orders below the smallest recorded floor (3.0e-6) and never surfaces
in the census; (iv) the "classical Bertsekas rollout improvement
property" label is appropriate: the hypothesis the classical argument
needs is sequential consistency of the base heuristic, which is exactly
what "deterministic function of the revealed SET" delivers, and which
(i) verifies for the code as shipped.

**S7. The 0.499 fixed-point story (V6).** 037 part E's claim is
verified exactly: the four step-0 rollout scores of μ_Q are equal at 60
digits, the unrationalized float measure's step-0 gap is 1.617e-10
(record: ~1.6e-10), and roll(μ_float) = (1,0,2,3) ≠ (0,1,2,3) =
roll(μ_Q) — the certified order really must come from μ_Q. But the
*mechanism* 037 lead 4 asserts is wrong: μ_Q is **not**
exactly-exchangeable. Rationalization sends the 8.1e-11 atom `0010` to
weight exactly 0, leaving a two-atom measure {0000: ~0.501, 1101:
~0.499}; coordinate 1 has marginal exactly 0 and the stabilizer is only
S₃ on {0,2,3} (order 6 of 24). The 4-way tie follows from that
symmetry plus the inert zero-marginal coordinate — with two atoms,
every informative reveal splits the coupling completely, so all 24
orders give the same CR. Note what this measure is: a perfectly
*correlated* pair at weight q ≈ 0.499 with CR/H = (1−h(q))/h(q) — the
same ratio form as the product extremal, achieved by the opposite
dependence structure.

## Why it failed / what survived

Nothing load-bearing failed; this is a confirmation pass with
reporting-level corrections. What it adds beyond 037/038: the
certificates now hold under a *fully-rationalized* order derivation
(closing the one scope gap the committed fixed-point check left open);
(ROLL-DOM)'s hinges are verified on the code as shipped, not just in
prose; and the 0.499 attractor is correctly identified as the
correlated pair rather than the product — which sharpens, not weakens,
037 lead 4: the interesting question is now *which* of the two extremal
families the descent selects as cap → 1/2, and whether any measure
strictly between them can dip below both. Reusable: the memoized
history-tree evaluator (fourth independent HU/CR implementation, and
the fastest exact-Fraction one at small n); the stabilizer/support
diagnostic for descent endpoints (a one-line check that would have
caught Correction 3 at write-time); the perturbed-exchangeable stress
family for tie-tolerance claims.
